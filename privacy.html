Privacy Policy

PDF Reader: Embedded Audio · Last updated 30 October 2026

PDF Reader does not collect, store or share any personal data.

The PDFs you open stay on your Mac. The app reads and saves only the files you choose. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send anything over the internet.

The app remembers a few settings on your Mac, such as your window size and your list of recent files. They never leave your computer.

If you email us for support, we use your message only to reply to you.
