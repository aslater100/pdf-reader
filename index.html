PDF Reader: Embedded Audio

Audio in your PDF won't play?

Course packets and language lessons often have their recordings built into the PDF. Many were made for Adobe's Flash player, which no longer works, so Preview and Acrobat show an empty box where the player was. PDF Reader finds those recordings and puts a play button in that spot.

Free. iPhone and iPad version coming soon.

Every recording, on the page and in a list

Each recording gets a play button where it sits on the page, and the sidebar lists every recording in the document. Skip back to hear a line again, slow it down, or repeat a clip until you have it.

Why the audio stopped working

For years, the usual way to put a recording in a PDF was Acrobat's rich media player, which ran on Adobe Flash. Adobe ended Flash at the end of 2020, and the PDF apps people use today don't play that kind of media. The recording is still inside the file, but nothing opens it.

PDF Reader reads the audio straight out of the file and plays it directly, so it doesn't need Flash. Nothing is converted or uploaded, and your PDF isn't changed unless you save highlights into it.

What it does

Play

  • A play button on the page for every recording
  • A sidebar listing every recording
  • Skip back or forward 5 seconds
  • Speed from 0.5× to 1.5×, and repeat
  • Save any recording as an audio file

Highlight and save

  • Highlight text in five colors
  • Click a highlight to recolor or remove it
  • Highlights save into the PDF, so other apps show them
  • The rest of the file, audio included, stays as it was

Read

  • Search the whole document
  • Many languages, including right-to-left scripts
  • Light and dark mode
  • Works offline and collects no data

Plays audio from Acrobat rich media (Flash) players, screen and sound annotations, and attached audio files in MP3, M4A, WAV and other common formats.

Common questions

My PDF has audio but no play button appears.
Check the Audio list in the sidebar. Some recordings are attached to the whole document rather than a page, so they appear only there. If the list is empty, the recording may use a format the app doesn't read yet; please email us the file.
How do I highlight text?
Click Highlight in the toolbar, pick a color, then select text. You can also select text first and click the Highlight button that appears. Click a highlight to change its color or remove it.
Will saving damage my PDF?
No. Saving adds your highlights to the end of the file and leaves the rest, including the audio, as it was.
Can I keep a recording as a separate file?
Yes. While it plays, click the download button in the player bar.
Can it open password-protected PDFs?
Not yet. Open the file in an app that knows the password, save an unprotected copy, and open that.
